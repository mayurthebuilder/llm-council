# Northstar Signal: fictional B2B SaaS digital marketing launch

This is invented demonstration data, not a real company, customer, or performance
record.

Northstar Signal is a fictional workflow software company preparing a 90-day
launch for operations leaders at mid-sized professional-services firms. Its
product promise is to reduce the time teams spend coordinating recurring client
work. The company has no verified brand-awareness, search-demand, conversion, or
customer-acquisition baseline.

The fictional launch team includes one marketer, one content specialist, and
part-time design and engineering support. Its illustrative budget is USD 24,000:
USD 10,000 for paid experiments, USD 8,000 for content and creative production,
and USD 6,000 for measurement, landing-page, and technical improvements. These
figures are constraints for the exercise, not market benchmarks.

Create a strategy-first digital marketing launch that:

- sharpens the audience, positioning, and core message;
- balances paid tests, organic distribution, lifecycle follow-up, and content;
- establishes sound SEO foundations;
- uses answer-first, expert-attributed content for AEO;
- improves entity clarity, original evidence, and citation-worthy pages for GEO;
- defines qualified-demand KPIs, learning milestones, and stop/scale rules; and
- identifies privacy, attribution, compliance, and reputational risks.

Separate supplied facts from recommendations and identify missing audience,
competitive, keyword, search-platform, and conversion evidence. Do not promise
rankings, citations, traffic, pipeline, or revenue.
